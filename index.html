How it works

Five steps. One call to start.

You never have to sort a drawer, price a lamp or open the door on sale day. Here's exactly what happens after you reach out.

I

Free consultation

We walk the home together, talk about your timeline, and tell you honestly what to expect.

II

Sorting & staging

Everything is organized, cleaned up and displayed room by room so it shows at its best.

III

Research & pricing

Antiques, art, jewelry and collectibles get researched and priced to sell for what they're worth.

IV

Marketing & sale days

We photograph, list and promote the sale, then run it: staffed, secured and managed start to finish.

V

Settlement & clean-out

You get an accounting and a check. What didn't sell is donated or hauled, and the house is left clean.
